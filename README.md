### 💼 About & Core Engineering Focus

I operate at the intersection of **Data Science, Industrial Computer Vision, and Enterprise Data Management**.

In heavy industry and high-temperature metallurgy (such as **aluminum recycling furnaces, smelting operations, and continuous casting**), AI models cannot afford hallucinations or loose assumptions. Reliability depends on **deterministic sensor pipelines, radiometric temperature precision, and rigorous defect detection**. I combine **Python software engineering** with **DAMA-DMBOK data governance** to deploy robust computer vision and telemetry systems in harsh industrial environments.

* ⚙️ **Industrial Computer Vision & Surface Inspection:** Deep learning and machine vision pipelines for defect detection, crack segmentation, spalling diagnosis, and structural degradation in refractory lining materials and metal surfaces (PyTorch, Ultralytics YOLO, Intel OpenVINO, OpenCV).
* 🌡️ **Thermography & Thermal Radiometrics:** Processing uncompressed radiometric infrared matrices from industrial thermal imaging cameras (FLIR, Seek Thermal) to monitor shell skin temperatures, detect thermal insulation breaches, and map furnace hotspots in real time.
* 📡 **Industrial IoT & Sensor Fusion Telemetry:** Integrating telemetry streams across high-temperature pyrometers, acoustic sensors, thermocouple arrays, and SCADA/PLC systems via industrial protocols (OPC-UA, Modbus, MQTT).
* 🏛️ **Enterprise Data Management (CDMP®):** Grounded in the DAMA-DMBOK framework — enforcing Data Quality dimensions, Data Governance, Metadata Management, and deterministic runtime data contracts.
* 🌐 **Open Source Foundations:** Active open-source collaborator contributing targeted fixes, multi-chunk streaming engines, and technical documentation in foundational AI repositories (**LiteLLM**, **Hugging Face**).
